Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of sites and apps can be hosted here?

Static sites, CMS-driven sites, and application backends including Node.js services and APIs.

Can you manage my DNS and domain for me?

Yes — DNS zone configuration and domain guidance are handled as part of onboarding and support.

Do you provide SSL certificates?

Yes, certificates are issued and renewed for managed domains, with HTTPS enforced.

How are backups handled?

Backups run on a defined schedule with documented restore steps. Specific frequency depends on your plan.

Is there real-time monitoring and alerting?

Monitoring can be configured per service. This site's Status section is illustrative, not a live feed, unless a monitoring integration is explicitly connected for your account.
